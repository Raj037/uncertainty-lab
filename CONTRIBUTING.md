# Contributing

Contributions are welcome if they preserve Uncertainty Lab's fail-closed
scientific and numerical-assurance principles.

By submitting a contribution, you represent that you have the right to submit
it and agree that your contribution may be distributed under the project's
Apache License 2.0. Do not submit code, data, documentation, benchmark material,
or other content copied from a source whose license does not permit inclusion.

For scientific changes, include tests that state the claim being qualified,
reference or analytic benchmark where applicable, failure behavior, and any
scope limitations. A passing test must not be used to imply accreditation,
certification, endorsement, or scientific validity outside the tested claim.

Do not include API keys, credentials, private datasets, personal information,
or proprietary/confidential material in issues, pull requests, fixtures, or
commits.
